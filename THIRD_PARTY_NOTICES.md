# Third-party project assets

The generated projects bundle the official Gradle wrapper and WPILib templates from https://github.com/wpilibsuite/vscode-wpilib/tree/v2026.2.1 (WPILib BSD license). Gradle wrapper files carry their Apache-2.0 notices. Runtime dependencies retain their upstream licenses and are downloaded by the user's robotics toolchain.

Vendor metadata sources:
- https://software-metadata.revrobotics.com/REVLib-2026.json
- https://maven.ctr-electronics.com/release/com/ctre/phoenix6/latest/Phoenix6-frc2026-latest.json
- https://3015rangerrobotics.github.io/pathplannerlib/PathplannerLib.json
- https://github.com/wpilibsuite/allwpilib/blob/v2026.2.1/wpilibNewCommands/WPILibNewCommands.json

RobotForge is an independent tool and is not an official FIRST, WPILib, REV, CTRE, or PathPlanner product.
